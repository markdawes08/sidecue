## Problem and evidence

Describe the concrete requirement, observed limitation, or benchmark motivating the decision. Link evidence and current primary documentation.

## Decision

## Alternatives and tradeoffs

## Implementation and validation

Identify affected contracts, schemas, documents, tasks, and tests. Specify how the decision will be proven and whether existing evidence must be rerun. Do not call a proposal validated before measurement.
