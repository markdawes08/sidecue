## Limits and follow-up

The coordinator's safety depends on its effects satisfying the explicit nonblocking activation/fence/finalization and recovery-write-fence contracts. The current Windows capture run opens active, while this lifecycle expects an inert lease; the composed adapter and actual device behavior remain SC-010A. Recovery and queued-write integration are SC-010B. A non-cooperative durable-start operation may delay End while its ambiguous write remains unresolved; capture is fenced and disposal uses bounded deferred cleanup. SC-012A tracks resolving that operation and verifying restart behavior.

`IsCurrent` is a snapshot check, not an atomic UI publication. SC-016 tracks enforcing fences at the serialized publication boundary. Real persistence, transcript cutoffs, provider drain, summary-job transaction, incomplete-tail handling, and UI effects are not proven by these fake-backed tests and remain in their owning tasks. A late uncooperative open can stay pending until the adapter returns; further opens are rejected until it is safely cleaned up. The foreground identity set is bounded at 4,096 unique requests per session and rejects additional requests explicitly. No gate passes solely from this evidence.
