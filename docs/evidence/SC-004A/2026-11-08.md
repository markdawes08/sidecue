## Selection and provenance

The candidate is a Sidecue-built SQLCipher Community 4.20.0 DLL, statically linked with OpenSSL 3.5.9. [Zetetic's signed source archive](https://www.zetetic.net/sqlcipher/verify/) SHA-256 is `a1417a5a672a2342de6ae44e08ccd68487fa3d0a43212331f4226a0d00b51b9c`; its detached signature verified using signing subkey `A4EA79E649E745B92117A56F0CB99EE267FD0322`, with primary fingerprint `D83F5F9EB811D6E6B4A0D9C5D1FA3A2A97ED25C2` checked against Zetetic's published fingerprint. GPG did not independently establish web-of-trust ownership. The official [OpenSSL 3.5.9 source](https://www.openssl-library.org/source/) SHA-256 matched its published checksum: `603f5602e2eef00d77fbd429d34dcd5822bb301757a1bc9cdb24c670f1eb859a`.

[SQLCipher Community terms](https://www.zetetic.net/sqlcipher/community/) permit redistribution with the required license and copyright notices. OpenSSL 3.x is Apache-2.0; its applicable notices must also accompany distribution. This is not Zetetic's commercial Windows package. The managed canary pins Microsoft.Data.Sqlite.Core 10.0.14, SQLitePCLRaw.provider.dynamic_cdecl 3.0.5 and resolved SQLitePCLRaw.core 3.0.5. The old unofficial e_sqlcipher bundle is deprecated and was not used. The native candidate and full license/release considerations are recorded in [DEPENDENCIES.md](../../DEPENDENCIES.md).

SQLCipher 4.20.0 declares a SQLite 3.54.1 baseline, newer than the [3.51.3 WAL-reset fix](https://sqlite.org/wal.html#the_wal_reset_bug). The loaded source ID ends in `alt1`, which SQLCipher's source-generation tool emits when source files differ from SQLite's upstream manifest. A targeted comparison of upstream SQLite 3.54.1 `src/wal.c` with the signed SQLCipher source found only two codec-related changes in WAL frame writing; upstream SHA-256 `11ab3050c715b8d4b05f20c9030ea96ed70475080ba62fe3f60e441ac765eb47`, SQLCipher SHA-256 `928cb668ff02355c7dfb7cba24121912495d55eef95e3998ad9a71db9f5e427e`. This supports the fix's source provenance, not a reproduction of the rare race or proof about every fork change.
