That preliminary suite failed two of five cases. In one synthetic accumulated-overlap case, the old normalizer emitted 2,080 samples instead of the expected 3,670–3,680. The other three cases passed. The test fixture uses generated sample data, contiguous device positions and estimated packet intervals; no meeting data is involved.

The correction projects sample positions from the first estimated session anchor only while native device positions form an uninterrupted trusted chain. It retains the estimated flag and fences missing/restored positions, true discontinuities, timestamp errors and material disagreement with callback timing. The 100 ms disagreement guard is a conservative policy, not physical clock calibration. At a segment boundary it emits the supported pending prefix and reports any unsupported remainder as an unknown failure envelope; it does not invent filter-tail audio.
