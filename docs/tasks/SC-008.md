### SC-008B

**Write finalized transcript revisions idempotently**

Type: implementation. Dependencies: SC-001, SC-004, SC-008A. Gates: none. Interactive Windows evidence: not required by this leaf.

**Prerequisites**

- SC-008A schema; deterministic transcript fixtures and store interfaces.

**Scope**

- Implement one serialized writer with short transactions, atomic commit-version receipts and explicit amendment history.
- Deduplicate by source/epoch/event identity, reject conflicting reuse and keep partial projection state separate from finalized evidence.

**Acceptance**

- Duplicate finals commit at most once; late/lower partials cannot demote a finalized segment.
- Corrected finals require explicit amendment and preserve history; a receipt means committed storage rather than queued work.

**Focused checks**

- Replay duplicate/reordered events, partial-final interleavings, conflicting IDs and amendments; assert no invented final text or duplicate commits.

**Stop boundary / excluded work**

- No live capture integration, provider protocol implementation or dashboard.
