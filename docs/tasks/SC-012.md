### SC-012A

**Recover committed sessions after unclean shutdown**

Type: implementation. Dependencies: SC-007, SC-008, SC-010. Gates: none. Interactive Windows evidence: not required by this leaf.

**Prerequisites**

- SC-007/008/010 complete; disposable encrypted store and controlled test process.

**Scope**

- Persist lifecycle transitions and detect nonterminal sessions left by an unclean application exit.
- Reopen committed transcript evidence in Interrupted state without comparing monotonic clocks across process lifetimes or inventing lost audio.

**Acceptance**

- Restart returns exactly committed records and retained source/timeline identities; missing normal End does not discard history.
- Interrupted recovery makes unresolved gaps and the new process timeline boundary explicit.

**Focused checks**

- Kill a synthetic test process during capture and commit, reopen its store and compare committed evidence and lifecycle state.
- Bound or resolve an ambiguous durable-start operation and verify interruption recovery. The current lifecycle can delay End while a non-cooperative start write remains unresolved, although capture stays fenced.

**Stop boundary / excluded work**

- No automatic resumed capture, portable profile recovery or drain-timeout behavior.

Owned areas: `src/Sidecue.Application/Recovery/`, `src/Sidecue.Infrastructure/Persistence/`, `tests/Sidecue.Infrastructure.Tests/Recovery/`.

Parent coverage: scope 1; acceptance 1; validation 1.

### SC-012B

**Bound stop draining and preserve incomplete tails**

Type: implementation. Dependencies: SC-007, SC-008, SC-010, SC-012A. Gates: none. Interactive Windows evidence: not required by this leaf.

**Prerequisites**

- SC-012A recovery path; explicit stop/drain contracts and deterministic provider fake.

**Scope**

- Implement bounded provider drain on End and persist completeness without promoting partial text to final evidence.
- Retain an unresolved provisional tail only under the documented data rules when the provider never finalizes.

**Acceptance**

- Stop finishes within its configured bound and identifies incomplete ending/tail evidence.
- Cancellation or process death around stop cannot create a falsely complete transcript or discard prior committed finals.

**Focused checks**

- Use a nonfinalizing provider and deterministic clock; kill/reopen around stop/commit boundaries and verify completeness markers.

**Stop boundary / excluded work**

- No summary generation, fabricated tail completion or live-provider endurance testing.

Owned areas: `src/Sidecue.Application/Recovery/`, `src/Sidecue.Infrastructure/Persistence/`, `tests/Sidecue.Infrastructure.Tests/Recovery/`.

Parent coverage: scope 2; acceptance 2; validation 1, 2.

### SC-012C

**Expose safe retry and export after recovery failure**

Type: implementation. Dependencies: SC-007, SC-008, SC-010, SC-012B. Gates: none. Interactive Windows evidence: not required by this leaf.

**Prerequisites**

- SC-012A/B recovery behavior; synthetic committed/provisional data and explicit export destination.

**Scope**

- Provide recovery/explicit export of available committed data and bounded retained unsaved text through the recovery use case.
- Keep failed storage/error-reporting paths usable without silently replacing newer data or claiming unsaved text is durable.

**Acceptance**

- Retry/export remains available after failed writes with saved versus unsaved/incomplete evidence labelled.
- A storage error while reporting recovery cannot recurse into another falsely successful save or invent missing speech.

**Focused checks**

- Inject storage unavailability during recovery/error reporting and export destination failure; repeat a bounded crash/reopen case through the final recovery path.

**Stop boundary / excluded work**

- No general history UI or backup restore; share the export format contract without depending on the separately scheduled SC-011 UI.

Owned areas: `src/Sidecue.Application/Recovery/`, `src/Sidecue.Infrastructure/Persistence/`, `tests/Sidecue.Infrastructure.Tests/Recovery/`, `docs/evidence/SC-012C/`.

Parent coverage: scope 3; acceptance 1, 2, 3; validation 1, 2.
